# Editing rules: picking and cutting clips

Adapted from the Vibe Editing kit's clip-creation rules and hook taxonomy. Each rule exists because it
was a revision someone already paid for. Apply them **when you pick**, before anything renders. The
goal is a first cut that needs no notes.

## The rules

1. **Lead with the payoff.** The first words heard are the most surprising, relatable or quotable line.
   If the gold sits mid-answer, **reorder** segments so it opens the clip. Weak openers: greetings
   ("hola, ¿cómo están?"), continuation words ("y", "entonces", "también", "and so"), an abstract
   thesis, jargon.
2. **Cold-viewer context.** Read the script as a stranger scrolling. Every line must make sense on its
   own. Cut or ground any "eso / esto / él / like I said" pointing at something not in the clip. A
   Q&A clip must carry the question.
3. **One arc.** One topic, one story, one payoff. Two half-stories stitched together read as two clips.
4. **Finish the point.** Keep going until the speaker lands it. Never end mid-build, on "y otra cosa…",
   or on a trailing "¿no?".
5. **Earn the length.** Don't trim a strong moment to hit a number. Organic reels usually land at 20–45s,
   and the brand profile's `length` is the hard range. Thin and short loses to complete and a little longer.
6. **The brand person carries it.** On the brand's own channel, their voice leads and the clip ends on
   them, not on a guest or interviewer.
7. **No leading fluff, no filler.** Start exactly on the hook word. The builder drops hesitation sounds
   and closes pauses, but **you** cut restarts, false starts and repeated phrases ("déjame repetir eso",
   "como les decía") by leaving those words out of the segments.
8. **Never invent words.** Rearrange only what was said. A hook title can paraphrase; the audio can't.

## Hooks that stop the scroll (first ~3 seconds)

| Type | Shape | Spanish example (Maria's world) |
|---|---|---|
| Stat | a specific, surprising number | "El 78 % de tus clientes agenda con quien responde primero." |
| Stakes | a concrete loss they'll feel | "Cada mensaje que contestas tarde es una cita que se va a otra clínica." |
| Contrarian | names a belief and breaks it | "No necesitas más seguidores. Necesitas contestar más rápido." |
| Story | a specific moment with stakes | "Una clienta me escribió a las 9 de la noche y nadie le contestó…" |
| Question | one they want answered (and the clip answers it) | "¿Por qué tu competencia llena la agenda y tú no?" |
| List | promises N items, delivers all N | "3 sistemas que toda clínica debería tener en 2026." |
| Reveal | insider knowledge, specific | "Lo que nadie te dice de las reseñas en Google…" |
| Confession | personal and specific | "Perdí mi primer cliente por no contestar un WhatsApp." |

**Auto-reject an opener that**: starts mid-sentence, refers back ("como dije antes"), opens on filler
("eh, bueno, entonces…"), or is just agreement ("claro, exacto").

**Strong endings**: a one-line conclusion ("La velocidad es el nuevo servicio al cliente."), a
reframing question, or a soft CTA in the speaker's own words. The hard CTA goes in the post caption.

## Hook title (on-screen text, first 3 seconds)

- At most 2 lines, about 40 characters, in plain Spanish for Maria. It works as the cover text on Instagram too.
- It complements the spoken hook rather than repeating it word for word. It can name the payoff the
  audio is building to ("8 de 10 eligen al que responde primero").
- No medical claims, prices, drug names, or "before and after" when the clip may become an ad.

## Mining a long recording (Clips mode)

1. Skim the whole `transcript.md` once. List every candidate: line ranges, the hook type, one sentence
   on the payoff.
2. Score each on: hook strength, standalone clarity, payoff, and fit for Maria (is it about her business,
   her patients, her time, her money?). Keep the best, usually 3–8 per hour of footage. Don't pad.
3. Build each candidate's script as it will play (hook, then context, then build, then payoff) and run
   the cold-viewer read before writing segments.
4. The same moment can't feed two clips unless they're deliberately different cuts (e.g. a 15s ad and
   a 40s organic version).

## Polish mode (one short raw video)

Keep the whole talk unless something is clearly dead weight: a restart, a repeated sentence, an aside
that breaks the arc. Say what you cut in the one-line plan. Let the builder close the pauses.
